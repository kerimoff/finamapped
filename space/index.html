Fine-mapping quality control

Are your high-PIP variants really causal-looking?

Upload fine-mapped credible sets. finemap-qc compares high-PIP and low-PIP variants for features of causal variants (open chromatin, proximity to genes and splice sites, replication across studies) and returns an interactive report.

What gets compared

  • high
    Variants above the high-PIP threshold in at least one credible set: the likely causal ones.
  • low_all
    Variants below the low-PIP threshold in any credible set.
  • low_anchored
    Low-PIP variants from credible sets whose top variant is confidently resolved: the strictest background.

Reading the result

Good fine-mapping shows high-PIP variants strongly enriched for open chromatin, missense and replicating variants, and sitting closer to TSSs and splice sites. Weak enrichment hints at miscalibrated PIPs or poor resolution.
